# Imágenes del Hero

Generadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, siguiendo la skill
`imagegen`. Se convirtieron a WebP mediante canvas del navegador, calidad
0.88, conservando la resolución original de 1536 × 1024.

- `public/images/hero/hero-dark.webp`: noche con lluvia.
- `public/images/hero/hero-light.webp`: día soleado.

Las rutas se definen en los perfiles ES/EN. Para reemplazar las imágenes,
se pueden sustituir estos archivos manteniendo sus nombres. Son escenas
ilustrativas, no fotografías del lugar de trabajo de Nicolás.

## Prompt de generación: dark

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: software developer portfolio hero
illustration, landscape 3:2 composition. Create a refined architectural
editorial 3D illustration of a quiet desk beside a large window. On the
desk: one CLOSED slim unbranded laptop, pencils in a simple holder, and a
small stack of books with blank spines. No people. The window overlooks a
subtle city silhouette at night, with clearly visible rain on the glass.
Deep navy blue palette, soft cool blue window illumination, elegant
realistic materials with a gently illustrated feel. Clean uncluttered
composition, the desk and closed computer clearly visible. Full bleed
image, no border. No text, lettering, logos or watermark. This is the
dark-theme variant, companion to an identical scene in daylight. Save the
generated image so it can be copied into the project.

## Prompt de edición: light

Use case: lighting-weather. Edit target: the preceding generated desk
illustration. Create its light-theme daytime companion. Change ONLY time
of day, weather, lighting and related colors: sunny clear daytime outside,
blue sky, dry clean window glass, soft warm sunshine across the desk, pale
blue atmospheric city skyline. Preserve the exact camera, landscape 3:2
framing, composition, closed unbranded laptop, pencil holder, books, desk,
window frame, lamp, chair and plants in their same locations. Lamp off.
Refined architectural editorial rendering. No text, logos, watermark, or
additional objects.
